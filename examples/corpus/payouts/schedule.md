# Payout schedule

*Invented sample text for the quickstart corpus, not a real payout schedule.*

Payouts run daily at 02:00 UTC and cover everything settled before midnight.
An account in review is skipped rather than delayed, so the next run covers two
days.

The minimum payout is 10.00 in the account currency. Below that the balance
rolls forward. A balance that has rolled forward for thirty days is paid out
regardless of the minimum, because a balance nobody can withdraw is a support
ticket.
